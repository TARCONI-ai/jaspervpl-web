## What does "Core Update" mean?

A Core Update is an update to the core of Google's search algorithm. It does not focus on a single aspect: it reviews many key factors at the same time.

When we talk about SEO and how to rank a website in Google, it is impossible not to mention the famous Core Updates. But what are they really, and why do they cause so much fuss? Google's algorithm is a formula that decides which web pages deserve to appear first when you search for something. It analyses thousands of factors in milliseconds. The world changes, content evolves and users expect better answers. So Google fine-tunes its algorithm with these updates to deliver more accurate, useful and safe results.

## The difference between a Core Update and other updates

Specific updates (such as Panda or Penguin) focus on one area: duplicate content, toxic links and so on. Core Updates are broad adjustments that affect many sites at the same time.

### Examples of past Core Updates

1. May 2020: big changes for health sites.
2. December 2020: focused on content relevance.
3. August 2023: hit sites with AI-generated content that added no value.

## The goal of a Core Update

Have you ever searched for something on Google and found that the first results did not help at all? A Core Update tries to prevent that.

The main focus is that you, as a user, find relevant, original and trustworthy content. It also aims to penalise misleading practices and low-quality content. That is why Google moves down sites that use clickbait, copy content or simply add nothing useful. Your page can shoot up or sink like a stone if it does not meet the new standards.

## 🛠 What to do if your site is affected

- Review your existing content: check whether you are really answering better than your competitors. If not, update or remove it.
- Improve user experience and speed: cut loading times, improve the design and make it work on mobile. Experience matters, a lot.
- Focus on search intent: do not write for bots, write for people. What does your reader need to know? Give it to them.

## Tools to monitor a Core Update

- Google Search Console: shows whether your traffic changes, crawl errors and more.
- Google Analytics: compare the periods before and after the update to see the impact.
- SEMrush, Ahrefs and other SEO tools: find out which keywords you gained or lost and how you rank against your competitors.

#### 📣 Google's official statements on Core Updates

Google usually announces on its blog when it launches a Core Update and gives advice on how to handle it. There are no secrets, just hard work. In SEO, what was true a year ago may be outdated today. Stay up to date, read the official documentation and follow trusted SEO blogs. Being informed is key.
