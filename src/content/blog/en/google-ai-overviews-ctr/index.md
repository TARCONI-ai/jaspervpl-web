SEO is going through a major change with the roll-out of Google AI Overviews. These AI-generated summaries, which appear at the top of the search results, are fundamentally changing how users interact with results and, as a consequence, the click-through rate (CTR) of organic web pages.

## What exactly are Google AI Overviews?

Google AI Overviews are AI-generated summaries that appear at the top of the search results, giving short, direct answers to users' queries. Unlike traditional featured snippets, these summaries:

- Combine information from several sources
- Cite their sources directly with links
- Are generated dynamically for each query
- Are designed to give fuller answers without the need to click

### The immediate impact on CTR

Early data suggests that websites are seeing CTR drop by 20% to 40% for queries where AI Overviews appear. The drop is especially sharp for:

- Informational queries ("how to…", "what is…").
- Searches for a definition or explanation.
- Direct questions with short answers.

### Most affected sectors

- Educational content: sites that answer common questions or explain concepts.
- Health and wellbeing: information about symptoms, treatments or general advice.
- Technology and tutorials: step-by-step instructions or troubleshooting.
- Personal finance: basic financial advice or explanations of concepts.

## Short-term strategies to adapt

### 1\. Focus on transactional queries

Queries with buying or hiring intent still generate clicks, because Google AI Overviews cannot complete transactions. Shifting content towards these intents can help keep traffic.

### 2\. Offer value beyond the summary

Create content that cannot be condensed into a summary:

- In-depth analysis
- Detailed case studies
- Testimonials and personal experiences
- Interactive tools and calculators

### 3\. Optimise for citations

Since Google AI Overviews cite their sources, optimising content to be cited can bring visibility, though not necessarily direct clicks:

- Write clear, concise definitions
- Use structured data and Schema.org markup
- Offer up-to-date statistics and unique data
- Position yourself as a primary source

## Medium-term outlook

How user behaviour is likely to evolve:

1. Early phase: an overall drop in CTR while users get used to the summaries
2. Middle phase: a clearer split between queries that AI Overviews resolve and those that need more detailed information
3. Advanced phase: possible saturation and "blindness" to AI summaries, as happened with ads

## Conclusion

The roll-out of Google AI Overviews is a paradigm shift for SEO. While the initial impact on CTR is clearly negative for many sites, the change also opens new opportunities for those who know how to adapt.

Content strategies will need to move towards deeper, more distinctive value that complements AI summaries instead of competing with them. Measuring success will also have to change, counting not only direct traffic but also citations in AI Overviews as a new kind of visibility. This is a reminder that SEO has always been a dynamic field where adapting is a necessity, not just an advantage.
