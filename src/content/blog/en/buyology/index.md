Buyology is a book published in 2008 by the Danish author Martin Lindstrom, whom The Sunday Times called the "wonder kid of the marketing world". Lindstrom is considered one of the gurus of global marketing. An expert in branding, he has written books translated into 19 languages and read by more than a million people, such as "Small Data".

Besides writing, Martin is the CEO of Lindstrom Company, the consultancy he founded, which works for large companies such as McDonald's, Nokia, Nestlé and Microsoft.

In this book, the author presents a complex neuromarketing study that looks into human behaviour and tries to understand how we act at the moment we decide to buy something.

The book is the result of more than three years of research into the behaviour of 2,000 volunteers while they were shown different adverts, logos, brands and products.

This gives us a better understanding of our brain and makes us aware of how irrationally we behave as consumers.

**Products are made in the factory, brands are made in our minds.**

The main topics of the book are neuromarketing as a potential tool for marketing and advertising campaigns, the tactics companies use to reach our subconscious, and the role our brain plays in the buying process.

It is a short book for any reader, since it does not use complicated language or assume you are a marketing expert. I recommend it as an introduction to consumer behaviour, which is one of my favourite topics in marketing.
