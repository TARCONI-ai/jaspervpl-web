Deep Research is an advanced AI capability that searches, analyses and synthesises information from multiple sources on the web on its own, delivering long reports with clear citations in minutes. It's ideal for research work, finance, science, complex purchases or rigorous analysis.

> **Note from November 2026:** the models, plans and limits I mention are those of mid-2025. They have changed since.

## What does Deep Research do?

Deep Research works like an autonomous agent:

- It searches the web, analyses documents and produces reports with precise citations.
- It runs on reasoning models (such as o3 or o4-mini) and keeps iterating until the topic is covered.

| Platform | Availability |

| **Perplexity AI** | Free for everyone with a daily limit; Pro includes more queries. |
| **Gemini (Google)** | In Gemini Advanced/Pro; breaks queries down and produces reports with web synthesis. |

## What are its main advantages?

- **Efficiency:** it does in minutes what would take a person hours.
- **Precise citations:** every claim is referenced, which makes it easy to verify.
- **Depth:** it can find information that isn't obvious, thanks to multiple iterations.

## What are its limits and precautions?

- **Not infallible:** it can "hallucinate", producing incorrect information even with a citation.
- **Usage limits:** ChatGPT has a monthly Deep Research limit, compared with the unlimited use of the normal model.
- **No substitute for human judgement:** according to studies, no AI is 100% reliable, especially in critical contexts.

## Which AI is best? ChatGPT, Perplexity and Gemini compared

### 1\. **ChatGPT (o3 / o4-mini models)**

- **Pros:** smooth integration with ChatGPT, structured reasoning, clear citations.
- **Cons:** limited use, can be slow for quick queries.

### 2\. **Perplexity Deep Research**

- **Pros:** free, with solid citations; Pro includes GPT-4.1, Claude 4 and Gemma models; excellent for quick answers.
- **Cons:** less versatile interface for long syntheses; less iterative control.

### 3\. **Gemini Deep Research** (Gemini 2.5 Pro/Flash models)

- **Pros:** very structured synthesis, good prioritisation, multimodal vision, few hallucinations according to users.
- **Cons:** restricted to advanced plans; less flexible than ChatGPT.

## Frequently asked questions about Deep Research

### Is Deep Research a tool or a feature?

It's an **advanced feature inside AI platforms** such as ChatGPT, Perplexity and Gemini. It isn't a standalone app, but a capability that lets the model carry out complex research.

### Does Deep Research access the internet in real time?

Yes. It uses web access to search, read and synthesise information in real time. Unlike traditional answers, it **uses several up-to-date, reliable sources** to build its responses.

### Do the answers come with cited sources?

Yes. That's one of its biggest advantages: **every fact or claim includes its source** (links to articles, papers, official websites and so on), so you can verify the information easily.

### Does Deep Research replace human analysis?

Not entirely. Although it automates much of the searching and synthesis, **it doesn't replace human judgement**, especially in critical contexts or sensitive decisions. It's a support tool, not a final verdict.

## Conclusion

Deep Research is a qualitative leap in how AI does research: it doesn't just answer, it actively investigates, like an expert using the web in real time, and builds well-structured reports with verifiable sources.
